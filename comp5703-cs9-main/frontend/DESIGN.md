# Design System: Business API Repository
**Project ID:** Local product · confirmed visual reference

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The product feels polished, optimistic and spacious. Warm white surfaces carry the interface, while oversized black typography gives important decisions immediate weight. A soft cobalt-and-coral atmospheric glow appears only near the top of the home page. It adds depth without competing with catalogue content. Motion is purposeful: category cards expand and lift on hover or keyboard focus, while comparison diagrams remain still and easy to read.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Warm Repository Canvas (#F8F5EF):** The persistent page and sidebar background.
- **Paper White (#FFFFFF):** Cards, inputs, panels and elevated surfaces.
- **Editorial Black (#0D0E12):** Headlines, primary content and strong structural marks.
- **Cobalt Action (#173CFF):** Primary actions, active navigation, links, focus rings and comparison connections.
- **Cobalt Wash (#EEF1FF):** Selected navigation, calm notices and highlighted controls.
- **Slate Copy (#69738F):** Supporting copy, labels and secondary information.
- **Quiet Border (#E2E1DF):** Dividers and default component outlines.
- **Communication Coral (#FF5E5B):** A semantic category accent used only inside Communication cards.
- **Transformation Violet (#6426F5):** A semantic category accent used only inside Transformation cards.
- **Other Teal (#078A70):** A semantic category accent used only inside Other cards.
- Status green, amber and red remain reserved for success, caution and failure feedback.

## 3. Typography Rules
Use Inter Product as the interface family. Home-page headlines are very large, heavy and tightly spaced, with short line lengths. Section titles are bold and compact. Body copy uses a comfortable line height and muted slate color. Small uppercase labels use generous letter spacing to create an editorial rhythm.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Primary buttons use solid cobalt with white type. Secondary buttons use paper white, a quiet border and black type. Controls have gently rounded corners and a clear cobalt focus ring.
* **Cards/Containers:** Cards use paper white, 14–16 px rounded corners and whisper-soft shadows. Homepage category cards expand horizontally and lift slightly on hover or focus. Category colors stay inside their own icons, borders and subtle washes.
* **Inputs/Forms:** Inputs use white surfaces, restrained gray borders and cobalt focus treatment. The home search input receives a slightly stronger cobalt outline and diffused blue shadow.
* **Navigation:** A warm-white vertical rail stays visible on wide screens. Active items use cobalt type on a pale cobalt wash. Profile and Inbox appear after login. Profile contains My Chains and My Submissions.
* **Comparison:** Source and target panels sit horizontally with one thin, static cobalt connection. Small screens stack the panels vertically and rotate the connection. No moving dot or decorative branching lines appear.

## 5. Layout Principles
Desktop pages use a fixed 158 px navigation rail and a fluid content area capped at 1320 px. The home page keeps its headline, search and category navigation in one clear reading path. Four category cells share one row and redistribute width on hover or focus. Coverage and contribution actions occupy separate full-width rows. Below 1080 px the rail condenses to icons; below 820 px it becomes a compact horizontal header and all content flows vertically without horizontal page overflow.
